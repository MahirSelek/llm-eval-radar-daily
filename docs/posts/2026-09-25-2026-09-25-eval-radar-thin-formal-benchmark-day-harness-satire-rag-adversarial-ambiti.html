Methodology Risks

Comparability risk is elevated precisely because formal same-day benchmark artifacts are scarce. Forum-driven Gemini outages and entitlement failures can masquerade as capability regressions if eval pipelines do not separate transport/authz errors from graded model outputs. Any live leaderboard or continuous harness that scores only successful completions will silently condition on easier or differently filtered prompts during PREFILL_QUEUE_OVERLOADED or 403 incidents, biasing win rates and refusal metrics.

Leakage and construct validity remain open issues around the RAG adversarial endorsement request and the fictional-distress safety-eval thread. Adversarial RAG papers often risk training-set or retrieval-index contamination; until the preprint exists, teams should not assume clean held-out corpora. Fictional distress scenarios used for safety evaluation can still trip provider filters, producing missingness that is correlated with the very behaviors under measurement—classic selection bias in safety scorecards.

Judge and harness drift risk shows up in the Rubric+Jev integration framing. Composing evaluation platforms with secondary judges or rubric engines without locked versions, prompt hashes, and human-agreement baselines invites narrative overconfidence: a blogged “what I found” can look like a new standard while actually reflecting one author’s stack, one day, and one undocumented judge configuration. Treat such reports as hypotheses requiring replication protocols, not as drop-in replacements for established benchmarks.
