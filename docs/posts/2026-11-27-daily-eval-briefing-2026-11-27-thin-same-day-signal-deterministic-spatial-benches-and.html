Methodology Risks

Comparability risk is elevated on thin days because sparse signals invite over-generalization. ZipGate’s deterministic lattice framing only yields trustworthy cross-model comparisons if encoding, legality, seeds, and scorers are identical across runs; otherwise “spatial benchmark” becomes a family of incompatible tasks. Throughput claims labeled as short benchmarks create a second comparability trap: without locked sequence lengths, batching, precision, and decode paths, tok/s figures cannot be ranked against each other or against quality scores from different harnesses.
