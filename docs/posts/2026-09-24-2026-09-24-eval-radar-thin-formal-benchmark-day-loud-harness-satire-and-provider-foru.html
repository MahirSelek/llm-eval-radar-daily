Methodology Risks

Comparability risk is elevated today because the item set contains almost no shared tasks, fixed splits, or official score tables. Forum claims about Gemini 3.1 ‘showing out’ or GPT-5.6 wishlists cannot be aligned to prior runs without pinned model IDs, decoding settings, tool whitelists, and identical prompts.
