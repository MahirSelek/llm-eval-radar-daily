Key Takeaways

  • Treat fixed coding suites as provisional: SWE-Duel’s self-scaling duel design is a concrete anti-contamination pattern—generate tasks and oracles at eval time and let participant strength set difficulty.
  • Decouple perturbation tooling from leaderboard stacks: perturber’s stateless, identical-across-runs transforms make robustness deltas comparable across labs and CI harnesses.
  • Safety eval for crisis support must score substantive multi-dimensional help, not refusal accuracy; DistressBench’s 718-conversation design is the day’s clearest construct-validity correction.
  • New domain benches (GeoFitBench-LLM, constrained spatial editing, GeopoliticalQA) raise the bar on requirements elicitation, geometric constraint satisfaction, and document-grounded RAG—not just answer fluency.
  • Synthetic adversarial dialogue (MADS) and pause-level speech scoring shift agent eval from function-call accuracy and aggregate latency toward trajectory robustness and human-matched turn decisions.
